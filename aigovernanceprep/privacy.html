AI Governance Prep: Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 November 2026. Publisher: Compora Pte. Ltd., Singapore.

AI Governance Prep is an app for practice questions and mock exams for AI governance certifications.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no server. Everything you enter, including your answers, scores, flagged questions and mock exam results, is stored only on your device. It is deleted when you delete the app.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not receive your name, email address or payment details. The app only asks the store whether your purchase is active.

Children

The app is intended for adults and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@plainsailapps.com
