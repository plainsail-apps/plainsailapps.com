Chores done. Pocket money earned.

A chore chart and a pocket-money jar in one. Grown-ups tick the jobs, the week pays out by the rules you set, and each child sees their week on their own look-only screen.

Who it's for

For parents who want help around the house.

Parents who want their children to help around the house and learn that money is earned, without a bank account, a card or adverts aimed at kids. ChoreJar is for the grown-ups to run; children only ever see a look-only screen.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. ChoreJar is free, with no ads.

Do we need accounts?

Grown-ups sign in with Apple, Google or an email link, so both parents and a helper see the same family. Children don't need an account: their iPad joins with a family code.

Who can see the money?

Parents see balances and the passbook. A helper can tick chores and sees goals as a percentage, never amounts. A child's device shows their own week, and their savings only if a parent turns that on.

Does my child need a bank account or card?

No. ChoreJar keeps a record of what you owe each child. You hand over the money however you like.

Where is our information kept?

In a private database for your family on Google Firebase in Singapore, so every phone stays in step. Only your family can read it. Privacy policy

ChoreJar is a record-keeping app for parents. It does not hold money or connect to a bank.
