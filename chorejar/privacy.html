Chore Jar: Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 November 2026. Publisher: Compora Pte. Ltd., Singapore.

Chore Jar is an app for tracking children's chores and pocket money for parents.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no server. Everything you enter, including your children's first names, chores, ticks, balances and your parent PIN, is stored only on your device. It is deleted when you delete the app.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not receive your name, email address or payment details. The app only asks the store whether your purchase is active.

Children

Chore Jar is set up and managed by a parent. Children may tick off their own chores, but the app collects nothing from them: the names and records you enter stay on your device, and nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. There are no adverts and no links out of the app for children to follow.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@plainsailapps.com

Chore Jar is a record-keeping app for parents. It does not hold money or connect to a bank.
