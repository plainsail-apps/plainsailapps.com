House rules for the elf

  • Set up after the girls are asleep; the elf "flew back" overnight.
  • No flour, powder or cotton-wool "snow": use white paper, towels or toilet roll instead.
  • Keep the elf out of the terrapins' tank and away from anything hot or electrical.
  • If someone touches the elf: leave a sorry note to Santa and the elf comes back the next night.
  • Forgot to move it? The elf "was too tired from flying" and stays put one night.
