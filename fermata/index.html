Questions

Can I log more than one instrument?

Yes. Add each instrument once and pick it when you start a session.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and start. There's nothing to sign up for.

Where is my information kept?

Only on your device. We have no server and can't see it.

Is it on iPad or Android?

It launches on iPhone first, and iPhone apps can also be installed on an iPad from the App Store. Android and a full iPad version are planned.

How do I restore a purchase or cancel?

Restore purchases is in the app's Settings. Pro is a one-time purchase, so there is nothing to cancel. More help
