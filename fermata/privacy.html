Fermata: Practice Log: Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 November 2026. Publisher: Compora Pte. Ltd., Singapore.

Fermata: Practice Log is an app for timing and logging music practice.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no server. Everything you enter, including your practice sessions, notes, ratings, instruments, goals and settings, is stored only on your device. It is deleted when you delete the app.

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules a notification on your device; nothing is sent to us. If you export your practice log as a CSV file, it goes only where you choose to send or save it.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not receive your name, email address or payment details. The app only asks the store whether your purchase is active.

Children

The app is intended for adults and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
