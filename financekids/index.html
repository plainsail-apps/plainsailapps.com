Who it's for

For P1 to P5 children and their parents.

Children in Singapore primary schools, from P1 to P5 (ages 7 to 11), and the parents who want them to grow up confident with money. It's simulation only and never handles real money.

Questions

Does it use real money?

No. FinanceKids is a simulation. It never handles real money and never connects to a bank.

How long does it take?

A lesson and its quiz take 5 to 10 minutes. The daily challenge is three quick questions. Once a week is enough to keep the streak.

FinanceKids is an educational game. It does not handle real money or give financial advice.

FinanceKids is free during early access, on iPhone and iPad in Singapore.
