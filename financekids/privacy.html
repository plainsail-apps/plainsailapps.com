FinanceKids: Privacy Policy

Effective 25 November 2026 · Publisher: Compora Pte. Ltd., Singapore

The short version: FinanceKids has no accounts and never asks for a name, email address, date of birth, school, photo or location. Your child's progress, coins, badges and pet stay on the device. To find out whether children come back, and so a streak can be brought back on a new device, the app sends us four kinds of usage event and a copy of the streak, labelled only with a random code the app makes up on the device. A parent can switch the usage events off and erase everything from inside the app.

This policy covers the FinanceKids app for iPhone and iPad, published by Compora Pte. Ltd. ("we", "us") under the Plainsail Apps name. FinanceKids is different from our other apps in one respect: it talks to a server we run. This page says exactly what goes there.

What stays on the device

Everything your child earns is stored on the device only: lessons passed, quiz progress, XP, coins, badges, store items, the streak, the state of Finn the pet, the primary class your child picked (P1 to P5, or left unanswered), which sets how hard the questions start, and the day this device first opened the app. The device also keeps the parent PIN (as a salted hash, never the digits), your choices for the usage switch and the daily reminder, and a short queue of usage events waiting to be sent.

We cannot read any of this remotely. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

What is sent to us

Four usage events. One record each time:

  • app opened;
  • daily check-in claimed;
  • lesson completed, with which lesson;
  • quiz scored, with which lesson and the score as a percentage.

Each record holds the event, the device code, the Singapore calendar date it counted against, the time the device says it happened, the time we received it, and a second random code used only so that an event sent twice is not counted twice. Nothing else.

A copy of the streak. One record per device: the device code, the streak (how many weeks in a row your child has played), the date of the most recent play and the Monday of that week, and when the record was last updated. It lets a streak survive the game data on the device being lost, and it lets you move the streak to another device.

The device code. A random code of 24 characters, made up by the app on the device. It is not derived from anything about your child, the device or Apple's identifiers, and we have no other information to connect it to a person. It is shown to you behind the parent PIN in Parent Dashboard → Your data.

There is no free-text field anywhere in what we receive.

What we do not collect

No names, email addresses, phone numbers, dates of birth, school names, photos, location, contacts, advertising identifiers or device fingerprints. No third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking tools. We do not track your child, or you, across other companies' apps or websites.

The class your child picks implies an age, so we say it plainly: it stays on the device and is never sent to us.

Your device's IP address reaches our server, as it does with any website, and is used only to limit how fast anonymous requests can arrive. It is not stored with the events or the streak, and our request logs record only the method and path of each request.

Why we use it

The usage events tell us whether children come back and which lessons work, counted in aggregate, so we can decide what to build and what to remove. The streak copy exists so a child does not lose a streak they earned. We do not use any of it to profile your child, to target anything at them, or to make any decision about them. We do not sell it or share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Moving a streak to another device

Copy the code from Parent Dashboard → Your data on the old device and enter it under I have a code from another device on the new one. The app shows you the streak it found and asks you to confirm before using it. Only the streak comes back: lessons, coins, badges and the pet were never sent to us. The new device's own code is retired, and its records on our server are deleted at the same moment.

The server has no passwords, because adding them would mean collecting the personal data this app avoids. So anyone who knows a device's code could read or change that device's streak. Treat the code like a spare key and do not post it anywhere public. The usage events cannot be read back by anyone outside our team.

Where the data is held

  • Compora Pte. Ltd. publishes and operates the app, in Singapore.
  • Neon hosts the database holding the events and streak copies, in Singapore (AWS ap-southeast-1).
  • Replit runs the server the app talks to, in North America. Requests from the app pass through it to the database in Singapore.

Both act only on our behalf. Nothing in transit identifies your child, but we would rather you knew the data crosses a border than find out later.

How long we keep it

Usage events are deleted after 24 months. The streak copy is kept for as long as the device keeps using the app, or until you erase it.

Your choices

  • Switch the usage events off in Parent Dashboard → Your data → Anonymous usage data. It takes effect immediately, and anything waiting to be sent is discarded. Nothing in the app is withheld when it is off. It does not stop the streak copy, because that is what lets the code bring a lost streak back.
  • Erase everything in Parent Dashboard → Your data → Erase everything on this device. This asks our server to delete the streak copy and every event recorded under the device's code, then erases everything on the device, which starts again with a new code. It cannot be undone. If the device is offline at that moment, the device is still erased but the server records are not, and they can no longer be found without the code. Deleting the app has the same limit, so if you want the server records gone, use the button first, or email us the code before you delete the app.
  • Daily reminder in Parent Dashboard → Daily reminder. It is off until you turn it on, and only then does the device ask permission to show notifications. The reminder is scheduled by the device itself; nothing about it is sent to us.
  • Tell us what you think in Parent Dashboard → Tell us what you think. It opens your own email app, addressed to hello@financekids.app; the app sends nothing itself. If you send it, we receive what you write and your email address, and use them only to read your feedback and reply. Ask and we will delete the email.
  • Ask us. Email the device code to support@plainsailapps.com and we will tell you exactly what is stored under it, or delete it. Without the code we cannot find anything, because nothing we hold says which records are yours.

Children

FinanceKids is made for children in Singapore primary schools, P1 to P5 (ages 7 to 11), to be used with a parent. The parent area sits behind a PIN. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, of any age, and because we collect none we do not ask for parental consent. If that ever changes, we will publish a new, dated version of this policy and add a consent step before anything personal is collected.

The law

We do not believe what we hold is personal data under Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012, because there is nothing we hold that could identify anyone. We follow the Act's spirit anyway: you can ask what we hold, have it deleted, and we aim to reply within 30 days.

Testing with TestFlight

While FinanceKids is in testing, it is installed through Apple's TestFlight app. Apple collects crash logs and usage information from TestFlight and shares them with us under Apple's TestFlight privacy terms, along with the name and email address we used to invite you, and any feedback you choose to send. We use these only to run the test and fix problems in the app.

Security

Everything sent between the app and our server is encrypted (HTTPS). The database is access-controlled. The parent PIN is stored on the device as a salted hash rather than the digits, though a four-digit PIN can always be worked out by trying every combination, so please do not use a PIN that matters elsewhere.

Changes to this policy

If what the app collects changes, we will update this page and its date before that version is released, and say what changed. A change that introduces personal data will never be made quietly.
