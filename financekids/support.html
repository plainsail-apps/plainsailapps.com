FinanceKids: Support

Need help, found a problem or have a suggestion? Email support@plainsailapps.com and include the app name, your device and what happened. We usually reply within two working days.

Subscriptions

  • Restore purchases: open the app's Settings and tap Restore purchases.
  • Cancel or change a subscription: on iPhone or iPad, go to Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. On Android, open Google Play, tap your profile, then Payments & subscriptions.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their own policies.

Your data

FinanceKids is a simulation and never handles real money. Read the privacy policy.

FinanceKids is an educational game. It does not handle real money or give financial advice.
