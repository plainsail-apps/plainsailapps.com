Questions

Is it safe for my child?

Yes. There are no ads, no chat, no accounts and no links out of the game, and nothing is collected. The purchase and the difficulty settings sit behind a grown-ups check.

Do we need an account or the internet?

No. Open the app and play. It works offline.

Where is progress kept?

Only on the device. Each child's iPad keeps its own progress, and deleting the app deletes it.

How do I restore the full game on a new device?

Open the grown-ups area in the app and tap Restore purchase. More help
