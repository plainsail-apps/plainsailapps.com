Hop Home: Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 November 2026. Publisher: Compora Pte. Ltd., Singapore.

Hop Home is an app for a sliding-block puzzle game for children.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no server. Everything you enter, including puzzle progress, stars, best moves and the sound setting, is stored only on your device. It is deleted when you delete the app.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not receive your name, email address or payment details. The app only asks the store whether your purchase is active.

Children

Hop Home is made for children. It collects no information from anyone, child or adult: there are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no links out of the app. The purchase and the settings are behind a grown-ups check.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@plainsailapps.com
