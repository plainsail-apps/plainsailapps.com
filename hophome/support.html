Hop Home: Support

Need help, found a problem or have a suggestion? Email support@plainsailapps.com and include the app name, your device and what happened. We usually reply within two working days.

The full game

  • Restore on a new device: open the grown-ups area in the app and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the Apple ID that bought it.
  • Ask to Buy: if your child's Apple ID uses Ask to Buy, the purchase waits for a parent's approval.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple under its own policies.

Progress

Each device keeps its own progress, so two children with their own iPads each have their own animals and stars.

Your data

Progress is kept only on the device. We can't see it, so we can't recover it if the app is deleted. Read the privacy policy.
