Job Sheet Pad: Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 November 2026. Publisher: Compora Pte. Ltd., Singapore.

Job Sheet Pad is an app for job sheets with labour, materials, photos and customer signatures for tradespeople.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no server. Everything you enter, including your business details, customers, job sheets, photos and signatures, is stored only on your device. It is deleted when you delete the app.

Photos are taken or chosen only when you ask, and stay in the app on your device. If you export a backup file or share a PDF, it goes only where you choose to send or save it.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not receive your name, email address or payment details. The app only asks the store whether your purchase is active.

Children

The app is intended for adults and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
