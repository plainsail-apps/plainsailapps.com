Privacy Policy

Effective 15 November 2026

The short version: our apps do not collect, store or sell your personal data. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking. What you enter in an app stays on your device unless you choose to share it. FinanceKids is the one exception to "nothing leaves the device": it sends anonymous usage counts and a streak backup, described in its own privacy policy.

This policy covers the mobile apps published by Compora Pte. Ltd. ("we", "us") under the Plainsail Apps name, on the Apple App Store and Google Play:

  • FinanceKids (has its own policy)
  • AI Governance Prep
  • Chore Jar
  • Rent Receipt Book
  • Fermata: Practice Log
  • Job Sheet Pad

Information we collect

None, except in FinanceKids (see its policy). Our other apps do not have user accounts and do not send the information you enter to us or to any server we run. We do not use advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Information stored on your device

Anything you enter in an app (for example names, addresses, amounts, notes, photos or signatures) is stored only on your device. It may be included in your device's own backups (iCloud or Google), which are controlled by your Apple or Google account settings, not by us. Deleting the app deletes its data from your device.

Files you share or export

Some apps let you create PDFs, spreadsheets (CSV) or backup files and send them using your device's share options (for example email or a messaging app). When you do, the file goes only where you send it, and the app or service you choose handles it under its own privacy policy. We do not receive a copy.

Purchases and subscriptions

Payments are handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your name, card details or billing address. The app only checks with Apple or Google whether a purchase or subscription is active, so it can unlock paid features. Apple's and Google's privacy policies apply to those transactions.

Device permissions

An app asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it, and you can refuse or withdraw it in your device settings at any time.

  • Notifications — reminders are scheduled on your device. No notification data is sent to us.
  • Camera and photos (Job Sheet Pad) — used only to attach photos you choose to a job sheet. Photos stay on your device.

FinanceKids

FinanceKids sends us four kinds of anonymous usage event and a copy of the child's streak, labelled only with a random code made up on the device, and never a name, email or anything else that identifies a child. A parent can switch the usage events off and erase everything from inside the app. The full details are in the FinanceKids privacy policy, which takes precedence over this page for that app.

AI Governance Prep

Your answers, scores and progress are stored on your device only. AI Governance Prep is not affiliated with or endorsed by the IAPP or ISACA.

Chore Jar

Your children's first names, their chores, ticks and pocket money balances, and your parent PIN are stored on your device only. Chore Jar does not hold money, connect to a bank or send anything to us.

Rent Receipt Book

Property, tenant and payment details, receipts and your signature are stored on your device only. Receipts are shared only when you choose to send them.

Fermata: Practice Log

Your practice sessions, notes, ratings, instruments and goals are stored on your device only. A CSV export is shared only when you choose to send it.

Job Sheet Pad

Your business details, customers, job sheets, photos and customer signatures are stored on your device only. Job sheets are shared only when you choose to send them.

Children

Except for FinanceKids and Chore Jar, which parents use with their children, our apps are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we do not hold personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. (FinanceKids holds anonymous records you can ask about or delete; its policy explains how.) You can delete your data at any time by deleting it in the app or deleting the app. If you have a question about privacy, contact us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

If an app ever starts handling data differently, we will update this page and the effective date before that version is released.
