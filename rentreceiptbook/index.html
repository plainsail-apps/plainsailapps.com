Questions

Does it work in my country?

Yes, in any currency, and every receipt field can be edited. Receipt rules differ by country, so the app doesn't give legal or tax advice.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and start. There's nothing to sign up for.

Where is my information kept?

Only on your device. We have no server and can't see it.

Does it work on iPad and Android tablets?

Yes. The same app runs on iPhone, iPad, Android phones and Android tablets.

How do I restore a purchase or cancel?

Restore purchases is in the app's Settings. Subscriptions are cancelled in your App Store or Google Play account. More help
