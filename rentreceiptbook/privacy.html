Rent Receipt Book: Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 November 2026. Publisher: Compora Pte. Ltd., Singapore.

Rent Receipt Book is an app for rent receipts and a rent ledger for small landlords.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no server. Everything you enter, including the properties, tenants, payments, receipts and signature you enter, is stored only on your device. It is deleted when you delete the app.

If you turn on reminders, the app schedules notifications on your device; nothing is sent to us. If you export a backup file, it is created on your device and goes only where you choose to send or save it.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not receive your name, email address or payment details. The app only asks the store whether your purchase is active.

Children

The app is intended for adults and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@plainsailapps.com

Receipts are records you create. The app does not give legal or tax advice for any country.
