Common questions

How do I restore a purchase on a new phone?

Sign in to the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with, open the app and tap Restore Purchases on the upgrade screen.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google, not by us. On iPhone or iPad: Apple's guide to cancelling a subscription. On Android: Google's guide to cancelling a subscription. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store you paid through. Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com. Google Play: Google's refund page.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

Your data is kept on your device, not in an online account. In Rent Receipt Book and Job Sheet Pad, use Settings → Export backup on the old device, send the file to yourself, then use Import backup on the new device. Your device's own iCloud or Google backup may also restore it.

How do I delete my data?

Delete individual items inside the app, or delete the app to remove all of its data from your device. We hold no copy. See our Privacy Policy.
